# Samurai Custom Rules in the rule replay (2026-10-21)

The rule replay (scratchpad/rulesim) gained a log-driven Samurai. The real engine (RuleExpressions.cs, RuleSet.cs) runs the
user's Samurai rule files against logged pulls: positions, target, hits, deaths, enemy casts and other players' buffs come
from the log; the Samurai's actions come from the rules, pressing Gyofu every 20 ms. The Sage path is unchanged: the clear's
Sage run is byte-identical to the run recorded before the change (8742 potency/min).

Everything here is regenerated by `./sam_all.sh` (about 10 minutes; outputs in sam_out/final).

## Rule sets

File selection follows RuleEngine.FilesFor for SAM_ST_CustomRules (buttons Hakaze and Gyofu): the layers for the button in
name order, then the selected file, with "enabled" honoured at evaluation.

| set | folder | files that run |
|---|---|---|
| live | variants/sam_live (copied from the live rules folder) | DMU_Mit_SAM.json is a layer but "enabled": false, so only SAM_Lv100_SlowMAL.json runs |
| plan, slot 1 | variants/sam_plan (repo files, DMU_Mit_SAM.json taken fresh at 10:34 today after the Tengentsu rewrite) | DMU_Mit_SAM.json (layer), then SlowMAL; `--set=meleeSlot=1` |
| plan, slot 2 | the same | the same; `--set=meleeSlot=2 --hits=1008A96B` |

## What was built

Files are all in scratchpad/rulesim:
- SamSim.cs (new): the Samurai, an IRuleRuntime with every builtin of RuleBuiltins.cs.
- SamCommands.cs (new): the commands run / timeline / samgcd, plus `--transition`, `--gaps` and `--meleecheck`.
- SharedBuiltins.cs (new): the builtins shared with Sage.
- gen_symbols.py (new): writes sam_symbols.json.
- mk_samfix.py and mk_samfix_clean.py (new): write the fix variants.
- sam_cluster.py, sam_sweep.py, sam_table.py, sam_gauge.py (new): analysis.
- Sage.cs changes:
  - its builtins that have the same meaning in both jobs moved to SharedBuiltins;
  - SimSymbols takes a gauge resolver.
- World.cs: new methods only (OthersTime, OthersStacks, Heading, HitsOn).
- Metrics.cs: three helpers changed from private to internal.
- Program.cs: one line that hands off to SamCommands.

**Symbols.** gen_symbols.py parses WrathCombo's sources the way JobRuleSymbols reflects:
- Wait = All.Cease.
- The job class's uint/ushort constants, by plain name.
- Role classes, by plain name and by Role.Name.
- The job's Buffs/Debuffs, by plain name and Buffs./Debuffs. name.
- Every other job's Buffs/Debuffs, qualified only (DNC.Buffs.Partner).

Run on SGE, it reproduces the old sge_symbols.json except for that file's artifacts ("config", "healthPercent"). The old file is left unchanged.

**The engine's own compile errors check the builtins.** All SAM files compile; nothing is missing.

## Model assumptions

**Timing**
- **GCD:** 2140 ms with Fuka and 2460 ms without (= 2140/0.87).
  - Measured on the user's pulls (`samgcd`), the median interval between GCDs with Fuka up is 2146 ms on Oct 14, 2141 ms on Oct 17 and 2139 ms on Oct 19. These log-time intervals include about 5 ms of latency.
  - `--gcd=measure` uses each pull's own median instead.
- **Casts:** Iaijutsu and Ogi Namikiri are 1.3 s × (2460/2500) × 0.87 = 1113 ms with Fuka.
- **Locks:** instant weaponskills and oGCDs take a 0.6 s animation lock; a cast ends with a 0.1 s lock.
- **Weaving:** SageSim's CanWeave, with 2 weaves per window (the user's MaximumWeavesPerWindow setting).
- **Movement:**
  - Moving is World's burst model: each logged displacement counts as a 5 y/s run that ends at the sample.
  - A cast can't start while moving, and is cancelled if movement starts before its end minus the slide.
  - Settings used: step 0.25 y with a 700 ms slide, and step 0.5 y with a 900 ms slide.
- **Incapacitation (model fix):**
  - Sleep, Stun, Down for the Count, Confused, Petrification, Deep Freeze, Knockdown and Fetters on the stand-in, and the 1 s after a knockback hit, block every action and interrupt casts.
  - That time counts as downtime, outside the "target up" denominators.
  - The user's logs have 1.9-2.1% of up time like this, mostly mechanic failures (Sleep, Confused). The clear also has the party-wide 32 s "Down for the Count" that opens Phase 3.
- **Statuses** apply at the press. The exceptions:
  - Higanbana's DoT lands 1.4 s after the cast starts.
  - Feint coverage counts from 0.6 s after the press (Metrics.LandMs).
  - justUsed counts from the effect: cast end for casts, the press for instants.

**Range and target**
- **Distance:** to the target's hitbox edge, as Wrath computes it: centre distance − 0.5 − target radius. inMelee is 3 y (MeleeOffset 0).
- **Hitbox radius (model fix):**
  - The log's radius is wrong for two of the clear's Kefkas: Kefka Says 0 y and Kefka Reimagined 6 y. The Reaper landed melee weaponskills from up to 8 y and 5 y away from them.
  - Fix: for any enemy, the radius becomes the 98th percentile of the party's 3 y weaponskill hits (centre distance − 3.5), when that is more than 1 y above the log's value. That gives P4 Kefka 5.7 y and P5 Kefka 7.8 y.
  - On bosses with a correct radius the same estimate lands within 0.7 y. The user's nights are untouched except one Kefka per night.
  - Before the fix, P4 showed 101 s out of melee and 26-31 Enpis; after it, 0 s.
- **Target (model fix):**
  - The stand-in's target is the primary target of its last damaging action within 15 s. An AoE that also hit the current target keeps it. Otherwise the biggest targetable boss.
  - The Reaper's Communio, Sacrificium, Harvest Moon and Gluttony list Exdeath first in Phase 3, which flipped the old target choice and ended Higanbana on Chaos. Phase 3 Higanbana uptime went from 52% to 79%.
  - World.Target, which the Sage uses, is unchanged.
  - A cast whose target is gone or untargetable at completion is lost.

**Positionals**
- Wrath's AngleToTarget, using the target's track heading and the stand-in's position: front 315-45°, flank 45-135° and 225-315°, rear 135-225°.
- needsPositionals is false only under Directional Disregard (3808). In the clear that is Kefka in the last 28 s; there is none on the user's nights.
- True North covers a positional.
- **Validated:** the 21 line's damage bonus byte (61 = positional hit, 56 = missed) agrees with the replay's model on 2,020 of the user's 2,050 Gekko/Kasha (98.5%).

**Gauge and state** (checked against the user's 31-line gauge, sam_out/kenki_check.py and combo_check.py)

- **Not modelled:** auto-attacks.
- **"Effective potency"** = potency × World.RaidBuffs multiplier × the crit/DH expectation relative to an unbuffed normal hit.
  - Base stats: 27% crit at ×1.62, 25% direct hit.
  - Battle Litany, Chain Stratagem, Battle Voice and Devilment (on the partner) are applied as crit/DH rates; World's flat 2.5% stand-ins for them are divided out of its multiplier.
  - Auto-crits (Midare, Tendo, both Kaeshi Setsugekkas, Ogi, Kaeshi Namikiri) count ×1.62 × (1 + bonus crit × 0.62).
- **"Raw"** = potency × the World multiplier.
- **Higanbana:** ticks snapshot at landing and tick while the target is present and targetable. Per the brief the DoT is lost when the target changes; `--dot=keep` keeps it (clear: 19,390 → 19,664 effective/min).

**Mitigation**
- **Tengentsu** (the coordinator's model, shared with teng_opt.py):
  - The status runs 4.6 s from the press (log time).
  - The first damaging non-auto hit on the hits actor consumes it. One ability's hits within 0.15 s count as one hit; hits over 150% of HP are mis-decoded and dropped.
  - That hit grants Foresight (9 s) and +10 Kenki.
  - "HP saved" = 10% of the caught hit + 10% of every hit in the next 9 s + three regen ticks of 5.5%, each capped by the logged HP deficit.
- **Feint:** 15 s on the current target.
  - Slots are the sheet's D1 (column 17) and D2 (column 19) Feint entries, including ➔ carries, matched to hits with reconcile.py's per-phase offset fit.
  - A "(Chaos)" slot needs the Feint on Chaos.

**Raid windows**
- Spans of at least 3 s where World.RaidBuffs counts one or more buffs on the player, or Mug/Dokumori/Chain on the target.
- "Under all buffs" means the pull's maximum count: 3 in the clear, where the Reaper's own Arcane Circle drops out.

**Stand-in for the clear (the clear has no Samurai)**
- The Reaper "Meowth That'sright" (100E572D, the sheet's D1/M1) supplies position, movement, target, uptime, deaths and hits.
- For meleeSlot=2, the Summoner "Shiro Yukihime" (1008A96B, the M2 slot) supplies the hits for Tengentsu and damage taken (`--hits=`); everything else still comes from the Reaper.
- Two effects of using a Reaper:
  - **Cancels are inflated.** The Reaper's movement was timed around its own GCDs.
  - **Positional misses are inflated.** The Reaper stands for Gibbet/Gallows, not for Gekko/Kasha. The rules' True North is nearly maxed (25 uses).

## Phase 1 → Phase 2: why the rules derail, and fixes (38 user pulls that reach Phase 2)

`run "<oct14>;<oct17>;<oct19>" sam --transition` records, for each pull:
- the rules' state at the last P1 GCD and at the first P2 GCD;
- the first 90 s of Phase 2: GCD sequence, Meikyo/Tendo/Higanbana/Ogi times;
- the first P2 raid window's auto-crits;
- what ran out or was wasted, from 30 s before Phase 2 to 90 s into it.

Per-pull blocks: sam_out/final/trans_live.txt and trans_fix.txt. CSVs: trans_*.csv. Clusters: sam_cluster.py → clusters.txt.

The transition is regular in the replay too: P2 starts at about 3:18, the first P2 GCD comes at +10.3-11.0 s, and the first P2 raid window runs from +31 to +36 s until +54 to +60 s.

The mechanisms, with examples:
1. **"Meikyo after downtime" burns the raid burst's charge.**
   - Example: oct14#8, Fuka expired at +24.9, so Meikyo went at +24.9 and Tendo at +29.4, ahead of the 4:02 buffs.
   - Fuka/Fugetsu run out because the combo priority picks the step by missing Sen (`!HasGetsu`/`!HasKa`), not by the buff timers. Carrying Ka into a 10 s gap lets Fuka lapse.
2. **"Raid burst Meikyo (deadline)" fires at the last moment that fits.**
   - Movement, "finisherNext", or a natural 3-Sen Midare going first pushes Tendo and Ogi past the end of the buffs.
   - Example: oct17#9. Midare at 4:12 used the Sen, the deadline Meikyo went at 4:13.4, there was 6.3 s out of range, and Tendo came at 4:29.2 with the window over at 4:23.5.
3. **Ogi Namikiri Ready (Ikishoten on cooldown, around 4:01) expires** because Ogi sits below Tendo Kaeshi, Midare and the Meikyo finishers. Example: oct17#9, expired at 4:31.6.
4. **A Meikyo burst at P1's end** loses stacks in the gap and delays the next charge.

The fixes (each behind a define in variants/sam_fix; mk_samfix.py writes it; baked into variants/sam_fix_clean):
- **fix1:** "Meikyo after downtime" only when `chargeForRaid`, i.e. a charge will still be back for the next raid burst.
- **fix2:** at a combo starter, "Shifu (Fuka would run out)" and "Jinpu (Fugetsu would run out)" when the buff has under 4 GCDs left.
- **fix3:** the deadline rule gets 2 GCDs of slack: `raidDeadline <= 3 * gcd` instead of `<= gcd`.
- **fix4:** inside a raid window with 3 Sen and the burst not done, Meikyo now, so the next GCD is Tendo Setsugekka; Midare waits for it.
  - A first version also held Midare up to 4 s before the window; that cost 0.6% in Phase 1 and was dropped.
- **fix5:** "Ogi Namikiri (before Ogi Namikiri Ready runs out)" as the first GCD rule, and ogiNow also fires inside a raid window that is about to end, even without Tendo.
- **fix6 (Dancing Mad only, not recommended):** no Burst Meikyo, Meikyo for Higanbana or Higanbana in the 20 s after the third Mystery Magic. It moves damage from P1's end into P2 but costs 0.4% overall.

## Limits

- **The stand-in:**
  - Cancels are inflated: the movement was timed for someone else's GCDs.
  - Positional misses are inflated: the Reaper stands for Reaper positionals.
  - The target choice is the Reaper's.
  - Melee range is the Reaper's: 3-5 y idle gaps where a Samurai would close in.
- **One pull:** single-run differences of ±2% are noise (the movement sweep spans 451 potency/min). Use the sweep, and the 38-pull set for alignment.
- **Not modelled:**
  - auto-attacks;
  - Gyoten's dash;
  - Enhanced Enpi;
  - Kenki from Meditate;
  - human reaction time and latency (5-6% of the user's up time);
  - server tick phase for DoTs;
  - the 0.6 s status application delay for self buffs.
- **Simplified:**
  - crit/DH stats fixed at 27% / 25%;
  - raid-buff values are World's rough multipliers;
  - Feint/Tengentsu mitigation is counted per hit on one player, and Feint isn't checked against the hit's source (helper vs boss);
  - the raid-window "under all buffs" uses the pull's maximum count.
- **Higanbana is lost on a target change (the brief).** The game keeps it on the old target: `--dot=keep` gives +1.4% on the clear.
- **Countdown assumed:** the rules see a countdown from 16 s (pre-pull Meikyo at −14 s, Sprint, True North).
