# White Mage Custom Rules in the rule replay (2026-10-21)

The rule replay (scratchpad/rulesim) gained a log-driven White Mage. The real engine (RuleExpressions.cs, RuleSet.cs, linked
from the repo) runs the White Mage rule files against logged Dancing Mad pulls, pressing Glare III every 20 ms. Positions,
target, movement, hits, deaths, enemy casts and other players' buffs come from the log; the White Mage's actions come from
the rules. Only `WHM_ST_DPS.json` was optimised; `DMU_Mit_WHM.json`, `WHM_ST_Heal.json` and `WHM_AoE_Heal.json` are the
repo's, byte for byte.

- Deliverable: `variants/whm_final/WHM_ST_DPS.json` (and the other three files, unchanged). Diff against the repo file:
  `whm_out/final_vs_repo.diff`.
- Everything below is regenerated by `./whm_all.sh` (about 15 minutes; outputs in `whm_out/`).
- The plugin's own engine (RuleEngine.CompileText in the Release WrathCombo.dll of 13:11 today, not rebuilt) compiles all
  four final files without errors (`whm_out/plugin_compile.txt`, tool `scratchpad/whmcheck`).

## What was built (scratchpad/rulesim)

- `WhmSim.cs` (new): the White Mage, an IRuleRuntime with every builtin of RuleBuiltins.cs that the four WHM files use.
- `WhmCommands.cs` (new): `run`, `timeline`, `calibrate`, `pulls` for `--job=whm` (or the pulls spec `whm`), with the
  sheet slots, big-hit mitigation, healing counterfactual, per-phase table, plan presses, idle reasons, `--counts`.
- `whm_symbols.json` (gen_symbols.py WHM: WHM.cs/WHM_Helper.cs constants, RoleActions, every job's Buffs/Debuffs).
- `Program.cs`: one dispatch line (`WhmCommands.Handles`). `RuleSim.csproj`: the `whm_symbols.json` copy line.
- `Metrics.cs`, generalised without changing the Sage's output:
  - `LoadSheet(dir, col = 15)` (column 9 = White Mage),
  - `SlotsWith(world, sheet, check)` (the Sage's `Slots` now calls it),
  - `Healing(world, heals, hpPerPot)` and `Breakdown(world, heals, hpPerPot)` over any heal list (the Sage overload calls it),
  - `CalibrationSample(world, table)`.
- Scripts: `whm_ev.sh` (one summary line per run), `whm_sweep.py` (7 movement settings), `whm_table.py`, `whm_slots.py`
  (sheet slots aggregated, with the nearest press for each miss), `whm_validate.py` (cross-check with report.py),
  `whm_rulediff.py` (outside-Dancing-Mad check), `mkwhm.py` (every candidate edit, by name), `whm_all.sh`.
- `scratchpad/actshape/desc2` (copy of Desc with cast time, cost, charges, ranges) for the game data below;
  `scratchpad/whmcheck` (compiles rule files with the plugin's engine).

**Regression.** Before any shared file changed, four runs were captured (`whm_out/regress_before`): the clear's Sage
(`run <clear> sge --me=100EC7EB --slots --shields --heal`), the clear's Samurai (`run <clear> dmu --job=sam --me=100E572D
--set=optMeleeSlot=2 --hits=1008A96B --detail`), the Oct 20 Sage set (`run <Oct 20> sge --heal`) and the Oct 19 Samurai set
(`run oct19.log sam --rules=variants/sam_live`). After the changes all four are byte-identical (`whm_out/regress_check`).

- **Gauge.** Lily 0-3, one per 20 s in combat while alive, the timer stopped at 3; Blood Lily 0-3; `gauge(LilyTimer)` = ms
  **elapsed** towards the next Lily (0..20,000), as Wrath reads it (WHM.cs:253 `20000 - gauge.LilyTimer`,
  WHM_Helper.cs:300 `LilyTimer >= 12000`); Dalamud's XML doc says "time to next lily"; the replay follows Wrath. Remote
  players' gauges are not in the log (type 31 lines are the log owner's only), so this cannot be checked from these logs.
- **Duty start: 3 Lilies and 3 Blood Lilies** (7.4). Every reference player fires Afflatus Misery 3-7 s into every pull,
  recommences after wipes included, with no Lily spent before (`whm_out/pulls_*.txt --opener`, report.py Misery counts =
  floor(Lilies / 3) + 1 per pull). Death empties Lilies and Blood Lilies (the guide: Lilies are lost on death) and your
  statuses; MP is kept.
- **Timing** as the Samurai replay: 20 ms steps, countdown from -16 s, 0.6 s locks after instants and oGCDs, 0.1 s after a
  cast, CanWeave as SageSim (2 weaves); a cast can't start while moving and is cancelled by movement before its end minus
  the slide; MP is charged when a cast completes; casts lost when the target is gone; incapacitating statuses and the 1 s
  after a knockback block actions and count as downtime. The prepull Glare (countdown 2.3 s) lands at -0.8 s and counts at 0.
- **GCD**: the median start-to-start of consecutive Glare III casts (2.2-2.7 s) pooled over the selected pulls (short pulls
  alone have too few): Nesta 2400 ms (Sep 29) / 2405 (Oct 2), Lunar Cry 2449 / 2447, Ba Bi 2355, H'ani 2325, the clear's
  AST (Fall Malefic) 2450. Cast time 1.5 s x GCD/2.5 s; Presence of Mind x0.8 on both.
- **Dia** keyed by actor: no ticks while the boss is untargetable or gone; Phase 2 Kefka is a new actor, so Dia starts over.
- **Raid buffs**: World.RaidBuffs (party buffs on the player, Mug/Dokumori/Chain on the target); the multiplier is the
  flat table in World.cs (no crit/DH model). The clear's stand-in's own Divination does not count (its source is the
  replayed player) - realistic for a White Mage party, which has no Divination.
- **Barriers** (Divine Caress, Divine Benison) take effect 0.6 s after the press (model fix found in this work: without it a
  Caress pressed 0.3 s before Mystery Magic's Flagrant Fire III was "eaten" by it, and the Wave Cannon slot read 64/104
  instead of 98/104) and absorb one non-auto hit (or a tank's second auto); Divine Aura starts when the barrier ends.
- **Targets**: SamSim.TargetAt (the primary target of the player's last damaging action within 15 s, else the biggest
  targetable boss); heal targets: the lowest-HP living party member within 30 y; tank targets: the lowest-HP tank.
- **Stubs** as the other replays: `tankbuster` 0, `raidwide` 0, `raidwideIn` 99, `stackMarker`/`canEsuna`/`healCleansable` 0.
- **Healing**: the rules' heals are scored, HP never changes; HP per potency calibrated on the logged player's direct heals
  (Rapture 400, Solace 800, Cure II 800, Cure III 600, Tetragrammaton 700; Benefic II, Helios, Lady of Crowns ... for the
  AST stand-in): 95-108 HP per potency.
- **Metrics**: sheet slots = the Ikuya White Mage column (Temperance, Plenary Indulgence, Divine Caress, Liturgy of the Bell,
  "Assist Tanks" = Aquaveil or Divine Benison on a tank) matched to the pull's hits with one clock offset per phase
  (Metrics.SlotsWith, as reconcile.py); the rules' effect counts from 0.6 s after the press; a Divine Caress slot needs the
  barrier unbroken on 3+ party members; the logged player's statuses (Temperance 1873, Confession, Divine Caress, Bell,
  Aquaveil/Benison on a tank) for the "logged" column.

## Limits

- **Movement** is over-flagged for remote players (17-33% false cancels at step 0.25/700, 10-21% at 0.5/900); the replayed
  movement was also timed around the logged player's own GCDs. Rules that use more instants while "moving" look better than
  they are; the moving-Dia and Misery-to-move candidates were judged at both settings and on the 7-setting mean.
- **Healing** doesn't change HP; it is a counterfactual (logged HP minus the logged healer's effective healing plus the
  rules'). The Glare button casts no heal GCDs, so the lowest counterfactual HP is -128% for baseline and final alike; it is
  a relative check. Per set at single settings it moves by up to 5 points with timing (Oct 21 -62 -> -67 at both settings,
  but -64.6 -> -64.3 on the 7-setting mean). Sep 29 Lunar's lowest is -103 -> -106 on every setting: the opener Assize now goes
  out with the first buff (1-3 s, a full party) instead of at 9 s when that pull's tank was falling to 6% (the logged White
  Mage used Benediction; the tank is dead in both counterfactuals). The alternative "PoM early, Assize at 9 s" keeps -103 for
  -0.25% potency.
- **Raidwide/tankbuster detection** is stubbed (`raidwideIn` 99, `tankbuster` 0): the file's non-Dancing-Mad raidwide rules and
  "Aquaveil for a tankbuster" never fire in the replay.
- **MP** is never binding (lowest 6,400-7,500) because the heal GCDs are on other buttons; Thin Air and the Lucid threshold
  can't show value here. A White Mage pressing Medica III often would see a different MP picture.
- **Dia** ticks every 3 s from 1.5 s after landing (not on the server's global tick), so the refresh point was not tuned.
- **Damage** is potency x the flat raid-buff multipliers (no crit/DH model, no potions); P3's second boss only through the AoE
  spells' extra targets.
- **One clear**: the Phase 3-5 backups rest on the 2026-10-05 clear (the plan itself was checked against it too).
- **Remote gauges** aren't logged: the Lily timer direction, the start at 3/3 and the death reset come from Wrath's code, the
  guides and the opener evidence.
- **Oct 21** is a snapshot of Network_30304_20261021.log taken at 15:28 (`scratchpad/whm/logs/oct21.log`); H'ani Bee played
  White Mage in pulls 1-8 and Astrologian after.
- Asylum's radius is the guide's 10 y (the sheet's EffectRange says 15); Bell procs ignore barriers.
